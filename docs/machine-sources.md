# Where each machine and zone came from

Moved out of `src/data/gymData.js` in the design pass (Spec 02): the app no longer shows confidence or source notes,
but the research stays here. "Exists" = how sure we are the equipment is at the DDFC. "Old location" refers to the
pre-Stage-A guessed map and is kept for history only; positions now come from `src/data/layout-*.json`.
