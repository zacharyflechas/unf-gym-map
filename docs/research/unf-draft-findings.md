# UNF Dottie Dorion Fitness Center: public-source findings

Researched 2026-10-20 for the `unf-layout-draft` branch of `zacharyflechas/unf-gym-map`.
I used only public web pages and photos. **No official floor plan, equipment inventory, or 3D/Matterport tour of the fitness floor was found.**

Confidence labels:
- **high**: an official UNF page says it, or a photo clearly shows it.
- **medium**: a non-official or older source says it, or it is a reasonable reading of a photo.
- **low**: a guess or weak inference.

Anything marked GUESS is not from a source.

## 1. Building and floors

| Fact | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|

| DDFC is 27,000 sq ft with 260+ exercise stations (2024: 275) | high | [ddfc], [ribbon] |

## 4. Other rules and facts relevant to the app

- **high:** Peak hours are 4 PM–close, with a 40-minute limit on racks. Only one barbell per rack. Olympic lifts only in a rack or on a platform. Farmer's carries with the diamond bar only behind the squat racks. Collars strongly recommended. [pol]
- **high:** Taking photos or video is prohibited without SWC manager authorization. So photos people send for layout fixes should come with staff permission. [pol]
- **high:** Free sessions are offered: a "Novice Workout" in the Cozy Corner that teaches the Nautilus circuit, and a free-weight workout. [ddfc]

## 5. What is still unknown

- The actual floor plan: building footprint and orientation, where the stairs/elevator reach the 2nd floor, the column grid, and where the atrium opening is.
- Which wall each zone is on: cardio windows, rack windows, balcony, Cozy Corner, Crossprey-Fit, Playground, main dumbbell area, the 4 light dumbbell areas, the 3 stretch areas, the Assessment Center, and the DDFC desk.
- Exact machine models and counts, and which 10–11 machines make up the Nautilus One circuit. Brands of the cardio equipment, the plate-loaded machines and the racks. Whether a Smith machine or a dedicated cable crossover exists.
- Equipment added after the 2024 renovation. The Spinnaker quoted staff saying "new and exciting stuff in the next few months", but I found no follow-up coverage.

## 7. How the draft map uses this

- **Zones:** Cardio, Outdoor Balcony, Atrium (open), Machines, Half Racks + Platforms, Cozy Corner, Playground, Dumbbells, Crossprey-Fit.
- **Labels on the map:** each zone shows "approx. location" (medium) or "location: guess" (low).
- **Per-machine data:** every machine has `confidence` (whether it exists), `locationConfidence`, a `note`, and `sources`. The detail panel shows all of these.
- **Removed:** Smith machine (no evidence). Cable crossover was merged into "Cable Station".
- **Added:** step mills, air bikes, upper body ergometer, Cybex Total Access, plate-loaded machines, hip ab/adductor, deadlift platforms, Nautilus One circuit, light dumbbells, stretch area, Synergy 360, TRX, barbell benches, monkey bars, Olympic platforms, slam wall.
- **Guesses:** lat pulldown, seated row, chest press, shoulder press and leg curl are marked low confidence. No source names them; they are typical machines and match "machines that isolate your arm and back muscles".
